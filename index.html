Straight answers · Questions people ask about AJH
Q · What is AJH Technology?
AJH Technology is a one-person web design, brand and video business in Suffolk, run by Harlyn Palmer. It builds websites for small businesses on monthly plans, and works as a monthly marketing partner for larger clients. Registered in 2024, trading since 2026, based at Creeting St Mary, Suffolk (IP6 8NG).
Q · How much does a website cost with AJH?
Three packages, all on a 12-month minimum: Starter — £150/month, nothing upfront. Business — £500/month, nothing upfront. Bookings & Members — quoted to your setup. The build, hosting and monthly changes are included. Whole marketing partnerships are priced on what's needed. Full prices →
Q · Who has AJH Technology worked with?
Upstream Works (website rebuild, brand and product launch film, as a monthly marketing partner), Wall2Wall Group (brand, website and print), Eastrada Facilities (single-page website and ongoing care) and Needham Market FC (brand refresh, club website and booking-request system, built as a volunteer).
Q · How does AJH build websites?
Mostly as static, hand-built sites hosted on Netlify and deployed from GitHub, which keeps them quick to load and simple to look after. Each site includes structured data so Google and AI assistants can understand the business.
