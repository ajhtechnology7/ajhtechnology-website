What does process help mean?
AJH Technology can help a small business tidy up how enquiries, quotes and admin flow through it — from the website form to the paid invoice. That can mean mapping the steps, connecting your website to the tools you already use, and writing simple instructions for whoever uses them. I haven't delivered a standalone process project yet, so it's priced up front once I know what you need.

How I'd approach it.

Look, build, hand over. You get a delivery date in writing before I start.

— STEP 01
Look
I talk to you about how work comes in and gets done, map the steps from enquiry to invoice, and point out where things are getting lost. You get a short list of what's worth fixing.
— STEP 02
Build
I set up or tidy the tools, connect your website to them, and write simple instructions for whoever uses them. I use tools you already pay for wherever possible.
— STEP 03
Hand over
I walk you through how it all works, in person if you're local, and help sort out anything that comes up once you're using it.
