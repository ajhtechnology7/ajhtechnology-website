What is included in the Starter package?

Starter (£250/month, £0 upfront, 12-month minimum) is for a business that needs to look credible and make it easy to get in touch. It includes:

What is included in the Bookings & Members package?

Bookings & Members (quoted to your setup, 12-month minimum) is for venues, clubs, sports facilities and hire businesses that take bookings or have members. It includes everything in Business, plus:

What is the AJH Technology delivery process?

It's simple and it's all done by me:

Talk: a free 20-minute call about your business, what the site needs to do and which package fits. You then get the scope, the price and a delivery date in writing.

Design and build: I design for phones first and hand-build the site in HTML and CSS, with the structured data, Google set-up and enquiry form included in your package.

Check: you see it working on your phone before it goes live, and tell me what to change.

Go live and keep it updated: I connect your domain, set up SSL and submit the sitemap to Google, then make your changes each month within your allowance. Bookings & Members builds also include the custom piece, such as booking requests and accounts.

Technology & approach

What technology does AJH Technology use to build websites?

Most sites are static HTML and CSS, hand-built, with JavaScript only where it's needed. They're hosted on Netlify and deployed from GitHub, which keeps them quick and low-maintenance.

Where a site needs more, I add it. The Needham Market FC site uses Supabase for its booking requests and accounts, and pulls its league table and results automatically from Football Web Pages.

There's no CMS editor. Your changes are made by me, within your monthly allowance.

Every site includes structured data suited to the business, such as LocalBusiness, Service and FAQPage, to help Google and AI assistants like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity understand it.

SEO & AEO

How does AJH Technology approach SEO?

SEO is part of the build, not an add-on. Every site includes:

Clean, semantic HTML, page titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, structured data suited to the business, an XML sitemap and robots.txt, share cards for social media, and set-up on Google with visitor stats.

After launch, your monthly changes can go towards improving pages for search, as well as content updates.

How does AJH Technology approach AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation)?

AEO is part of how I build every site.

I write key pages so they answer common questions directly, which helps AI assistants like ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity understand what you do.

I use structured data such as FAQPage, LocalBusiness and Service, and plain English about your services and where you work, rather than jargon. The Starter package covers the basics; Business and Bookings & Members go further.

What does 'AEO' mean and why does AJH Technology talk about it?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation. It's the practice of building websites so that AI assistants — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Bing Copilot — can understand what your business does when people ask them questions like "who's a good plasterer near Stowmarket?".

I talk about it because more people now ask AI assistants for recommendations, as well as searching on Google.

Nobody can guarantee an AI assistant will recommend you. What I can do is give it the structure and clear answers that help it understand a page.

Contracts & GDPR

What happens after the 12-month minimum contract?

After the 12-month minimum, your plan rolls on monthly, and you can cancel with one month's notice.

The site is yours once the 12-month term is complete. If you want to leave earlier, you can pay the rest of the term and take the site with you.

If you leave, I'll hand over the domain, DNS records and the site's files. Hosting can either move to you or stay with me as an add-on.

How does AJH Technology handle GDPR and data protection?

I take data protection seriously. Forms only collect what's needed, and enquiries go straight to your inbox.

Most sites are hosted on Netlify, and where any data is held depends on the tools your site uses. I'll tell you which tools are involved before we start, so your privacy notice can say so.

How AJH handles its own data is set out in the Privacy Notice.
