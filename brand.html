What's included in AJH's brand service?
AJH Technology's brand service covers logo design, an identity system (colour palette, typography, tone of voice), brand guidelines, and artwork for stationery, social media, print, vehicles and site. A brand refresh is included in the Business website package; a full identity on its own is quoted up front, in writing. Examples: the Wall2Wall brand and the Upstream Works brand pack.

How I build your brand.

Three steps. You get a delivery date in writing before I start.

— PHASE 01
Discover
We sit down (in person, ideally) and I learn the business: who your customers are, how you want to come across, and where the brand has to show up — online, on paper, on the van.
— PHASE 02
Design
I design the logo and build it out into a system: marks, colour, type and tone. You see it as it develops and tell me what to change.
— PHASE 03
Deliver
You get the identity in the formats you need: print-ready files, web-ready assets, brand guidelines, business cards designed and print-ready, and templates you can actually use.
