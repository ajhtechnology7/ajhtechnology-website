What's included in an AJH website build?
An AJH Technology website is a mobile-first, hand-built site with on-page SEO, structured data and a clear enquiry route on every page. It comes as three monthly packages — Starter (£75/month, up to 5 pages), Business (£175/month, up to 15 pages with a brand refresh) or Platform (£300 upfront + £350/month, for booking requests, accounts and integrations) — all on a 12-month minimum. The monthly fee covers the build, hosting, SSL, security updates and monthly changes; domain management is available from £15 a year.

Whether it's a one-page site or a full platform.

Built to load quickly

Slow pages lose visitors, so I keep images small, code lean and hosting fast.

◐

Mobile-first, properly

Designed on mobile first, then expanded to desktop. Most visitors are on a phone, so the site should work properly there first.

🎯

One clear next step per page

Every page has a single primary call to action. No buffet of buttons confusing your visitors into clicking nothing.

Easy for Google and AI assistants to understand
