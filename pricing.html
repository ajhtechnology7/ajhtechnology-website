Pricing questions answered.

Straight answers to what people ask before signing up.

Why is there nothing to pay upfront?

+
Most small businesses would rather spread the cost than find a big lump sum. The build is paid off across the 12-month minimum term, so Starter and Business have no setup fee and Platform has a small one.

What happens after 12 months?

+
The plan rolls on monthly, and you can cancel with one month's notice.

Do I own the website?

+
Yes, once the 12-month term is complete. If you want to leave earlier, you can pay the rest of the term and take the site with you.

Are there any other costs?

+
Hosting, SSL, security updates and your monthly changes are all in the monthly fee. Outside that: extra changes beyond your allowance at £60/hour (quoted before I start), domain management from £15/year, and any third-party tools you choose to use, such as booking or email software.

Why are you cheaper than an agency?

+
There's one person doing the work and no office team to pay for. You get the same person on every call, and the savings go into the price.

Which package is right for me?

+
Starter if you need one strong page or a small site. Business if you want the site and your brand to bring in enquiries. Platform if the site needs systems like booking requests or accounts. If you need a whole marketing function, that's a partnership. Not sure? Call me and I'll tell you honestly.
