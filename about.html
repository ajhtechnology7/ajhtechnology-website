AJH / About

Built locally. Built to last.

AJH Technology is a one-person web design, brand and video business near Needham Market, Suffolk. I build websites for local businesses, look after them every month, and make the brand and video work that goes with them.

Who founded AJH Technology?
AJH Technology was founded by Harlyn Palmer in 2024 and has been trading since 2026, from near Needham Market, Suffolk. It's one person. Harlyn does the work himself through three services: websites, brand and video. Every job is quoted in writing before it starts.
Harlyn Palmer Founder
From the founder

A letter, basically.

I grew up in Suffolk watching good local businesses with the same problem: their work was excellent, but their website didn't show it. The signwriting on the van was sharp. The website wasn't. First impressions were costing them work.

AJH Technology exists to fix that. It's just me — no team, no account managers. That keeps things simple and the price sensible. Every decision and every line that goes live is checked by me, and I bring the same care to a one-page site as to a full rebuild — for the trades, clubs and local firms that make Suffolk tick.

Outside work, I'm a first-team player at Needham Market FC. I was signed by Ipswich Town at seven and spent seven years in their academy, then went through the two-year football and education programme at the Needham Market Academy. It taught me a lot about turning up and putting the work in when nobody's watching. I bring the same head-down approach to client work.

No jargon. Clear contracts. Same person on every call. If something doesn't make sense for your business, I'll tell you that instead of selling it to you.

If that sounds like what you've been looking for — let's get a coffee.

— Harlyn Palmer

Three things I won't bend on.

The principles behind every job I take on.

01
You deal with me.
No sales team, no hand-offs, no account manager. The person you speak to on the first call is the person who builds your site and makes your changes afterwards. Email or call me directly on 07488 286207.
02
Prices in writing first.
You get the price, what's included and a delivery date in writing before anything starts. If something extra comes up later, I quote it before I do it — nothing gets added to a bill you haven't agreed.
03
Only real work on show.
Every project on this site is real — you can click through and check it. No mock-up clients, no made-up reviews, no numbers I can't back up. When there are results worth sharing, I'll share them.
